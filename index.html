100% Fully Automated WhatsApp Setup

Hands-free dispatching in 3 easy steps

By installing this lightweight extension on Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, WhatsApp Web will automatically click the green Send button and close tabs for every contact dispatched from Ostan.

1
Download or Locate the Extension Folder
Folder is already located inside your project at: whatsapp-autosend-extension
Or click: Download .ZIP and extract it.
2
Open Extensions Page in Browser
In Chrome go to: chrome://extensions
In Edge go to: edge://extensions
Then turn ON the "Developer mode" switch (top right).
3
Click "Load Unpacked"
Click the button "Load unpacked" in your browser, select the whatsapp-autosend-extension folder, and click Select.
🎉 You are all set! From now on, whenever you dispatch messages in Ostan, WhatsApp Web will click Send and close tabs automatically!
